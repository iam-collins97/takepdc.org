The website gives information about the championship and lets you download the registration form and contact us. We work to keep the information accurate but the official invitation letter and the registration form remain the main source of information.

02

Registration and Eligibility

To take part in TAKEPDC 2027 a school or institution must complete the official registration process. This means:

  • downloading and filling in the official registration form from the Registration page
  • confirming your participation by email to chamliho@gmail.com by 16 April 2027
  • paying the registration fee by 4 May 2027

Teams may enter in the primary and secondary (O-level and A-level) and tertiary categories. Sign language teams are welcome. The number of teams and individuals each school may enter is set out in the official invitation letter and the registration form.

Students under 18: registration for students should be made by a teacher or a parent or guardian on their behalf. By registering a student you confirm that you have the right to do so.

03

Fees and Payment

The registration fee is Ksh 10,500 or TZS 210,000 per delegate. The fee covers:

  • participation in the championship
  • the venue
  • accommodation
  • meals
  • a certificate
  • a tournament tag

Payment must be made by 4 May 2027. Please send your payment confirmation to us by WhatsApp or email so we can confirm your place. A registration is complete only when we have received both the registration form and the payment.

Please note: the fee does not include travel to Arusha or personal spending. Always use the payment details given in the official invitation letter and never send money to any other account or person.

04

Conduct and Fair Play

TAKEPDC 2027 is a peace debate championship. All delegates and teachers and guests are expected to:

  • treat everyone with respect and attack ideas and never people
  • listen to others and wait for their turn to speak
  • follow the debate format and the rules and the time limits
  • use honest and well-researched arguments and avoid spreading false information
  • take care of the venue and the accommodation and other people's property
  • follow the instructions of the organizers and the judges and the staff at the venue

We may warn or disqualify any team or individual who behaves in a way that is harmful or abusive or unfair. In serious cases we may ask a person to leave the championship.

05

Judging and Awards

Debates and individual events are judged by the panel of judges appointed by the organizers. The decisions of the judges and the organizers are final.

The awards and prizes are described on the Registration page and in the official invitation letter. We may announce the results and the names of winning teams and schools publicly during and after the championship.
