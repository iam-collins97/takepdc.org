Registration

Join the best school and college teams from Tanzania and Kenya at TAKEPDC 2027 in Arusha, Tanzania, from 9th to 11th June 2027. Below you will find everything you need to know about how to register, the team categories, the format and the awards.

How to Register

Schools and teams must confirm their participation by email no later than 16 April 2027. Send your confirmation to chamliho@gmail.com.

The registration fee is Ksh 10,500 or TZS 210,000 per delegate. All payments must be made by 4 May 2027.

The registration fee covers:

  • Participation in the championship
  • Venue
  • Accommodation
  • Meals
  • Certificate
  • Tournament tag

For any questions about registration you can reach Chamliho Martin on WhatsApp at +255 752 581 735.

Activities

Group Debates

Teams debate in the World Schools format in Kiswahili, English or French.

Public Speaking

Participants take part in public speaking as one of the individual events of the championship.

Spelling Bee

The spelling bee is held in English only and is open to primary school participants.

Poetry and Storytelling

Participants also compete in poetry and storytelling as individual events of the championship.

Team Categories

Primary

Teams from primary schools.

Secondary

Teams from secondary schools in both the O-level and the A-level categories.

Tertiary

Teams from colleges and universities.

NOTE: Sign language teams are welcome to take part.

Format

4 min

Main Speeches

Each main speech lasts four minutes.

3 min

Replies

Each reply speech lasts three minutes.

6

Rounds

The debate competition is played over six rounds.

Last

Impromptu Round

The last round of each day is an impromptu round.

Motion/Themes

The motions for the championship will be released one and a half months before the event.
