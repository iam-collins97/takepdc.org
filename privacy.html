Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This page explains what information we collect through the TAKEPDC 2027 website and registration process and how we use it and keep it safe.

Last updated: 29 October 2026

In Short

We Collect Only What We Need

We ask only for the details needed to register your school and delegates and to contact you about the championship.

Students Come First

We take extra care with information about students and never use it for anything unrelated to the championship.

You Are in Control

You can ask us to correct or delete your information at any time by contacting us on WhatsApp or email.

01

Who We Are

This website is run by The Living Smile International (LSI), a non-governmental organization registered in Tanzania with registration number 00NGO/R/5698. LSI hosts the Tanzania and Kenya Peace Debate Championship (TAKEPDC 2027) in Arusha, Tanzania, from 9th to 11th June 2027.

In this policy the words "we", "us" and "our" mean The Living Smile International. If you have any question about your information you can reach us on WhatsApp at +255 752 581 735 or by email at chamliho@gmail.com.

02

Information We Collect

We collect only the information we need to run the championship and to stay in touch with you. This may include:

  • School and team details: the name of your school and its country and the team categories you register for.
  • Delegate details: the names of students and teachers who will attend and any special needs we should know about, such as sign language support.
  • Contact details: the name and phone number and email address of the person registering.
  • Payment confirmation: the details you send us so we can confirm that the registration fee has been paid.
  • Your messages: anything you send us by WhatsApp or email.

You do not need an account to use this website. The site only remembers your light or dark mode choice while you browse.

03

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you give us only for purposes connected to TAKEPDC 2027. These include:

  • registering your school and delegates for the championship
  • confirming your registration and payment
  • sending you important updates such as the schedule and motion themes and venue details
  • arranging accommodation and meals and certificates and tournament tags
  • answering your questions and consultation requests
  • preparing the results and awards

We do not sell your information and we do not use it for advertising.

04

Students and Minors

Many participants in TAKEPDC 2027 are primary and secondary school students under the age of 18. We take extra care with their information.

  • Registration for students should be made by a teacher or a parent or guardian on their behalf.
  • We ask only for the details needed to take part, such as a student's name and school and team category and any support needs.
  • We never use students' information for advertising or for anything unrelated to the championship.
  • We do not ask students to create accounts or to contact us directly through this website.

Parents and guardians: if you would like to see or correct or delete your child's information, contact us on WhatsApp at +255 752 581 735 or by email at chamliho@gmail.com and we will help you.

05

Photos and Videos

During the championship we may take photos and record videos of the debates and the awards ceremony and other activities. We may use them to:

  • report on the event and share its results
  • promote future championships and the work of The Living Smile International
  • publish on this website and on our social media pages

By registering for the championship, schools and delegates agree that photos and videos taken at the event may be used in this way. We will always treat images of students with respect.

Would you prefer that a student is not photographed? Tell us before the event on WhatsApp at +255 752 581 735 or by email at chamliho@gmail.com and we will do our best to respect your wish.

06

Sharing Your Information

We do not sell or rent your information to anyone. We share it only when it is needed to run the championship. This may include:

  • The organizing team: the people at The Living Smile International who handle registration and scheduling and awards.
  • Venue and service providers: for example the venue and the people who arrange accommodation and meals for delegates. We share only what they need to do their work.
  • Authorities: if the law requires us to share information.

Results and the names of winning teams and schools may be announced publicly during and after the championship.

Please note: this website links to other sites such as WhatsApp and Instagram. Those sites have their own privacy policies and we are not responsible for how they handle your information.

07

Keeping Your Information Safe

We take reasonable steps to protect your information from loss and misuse and unauthorized access. These include:

  • allowing only the organizing team to see registration details
  • asking you to send registration forms and payment confirmations only to our official WhatsApp number and email address
  • keeping your information only for as long as we need it to run the championship and to keep our records

This website does not store your registration details. They reach us only when you send them to us yourself by WhatsApp or email.

A friendly reminder: no method of sending information over the internet is completely secure. Please never send us passwords or bank card numbers or PIN codes.

08

Your Rights

You are in control of your information. At any time you can ask us to:

  • tell you what information we hold about you or your students
  • correct anything that is wrong or out of date
  • delete your information when we no longer need it for the championship
  • stop using your information for a purpose you do not agree with
  • stop using photos or videos in which you or your students appear

To make a request, contact us on WhatsApp at +255 752 581 735 or by email at chamliho@gmail.com. We will reply as soon as we can. If you are a parent or guardian you can make a request on behalf of your child.

Please note: in some cases we may need to keep certain records, for example to confirm payments or to meet a legal requirement. If that is the case we will explain why.

09

Contact Us
