About TAKEPDC 2027

The Tanzania and Kenya Peace Debate Championship (TAKEPDC 2027) is a platform that brings together the best school and college teams from both countries to compete for the title. This year's theme is "The Clash for Fact: Misinformation and Disinformation in the Digital Era".

The Vision for 2027

A Shared Bond

In East Africa, Tanzania and Kenya share not only borders but also a rich tapestry of interaction. One compelling aspect is that both nations are home to the Maasai people, whose traditional lands stretch across the frontier, fostering a unique cultural bond. Their relationship, however, extends far beyond cultural ties into domains like economy and security.

A Shared History

Historically, Tanzania and Kenya gained independence around the same period, leading to parallel development paths. As members of the East African Community (EAC), they collaborate on initiatives to improve regional integration, infrastructure, trade and security. This cooperation is fortified by the fact that cross-border trade between the two nations accounts for a significant portion of their economic activity, bolstering their interdependence.

A Youth Movement

This creates a need to share this history with the youth and strengthen diplomatic relations and brotherhood through a peace debate platform. With over 60% of the population under the age of 25, the future of this peace rests entirely in the hands of the youth. TAKEPDC 2027 is not just an academic championship. It is a nationwide intellectual movement. As we navigate a world increasingly divided by digital echo chambers, economic pressures and social polarization, TAKEPDC 2027 will harness the energy of Tanzanian and Kenyan youth and transform them from passive consumers of information into active architects of social cohesion. By bringing the peace debate stage to every corner of the two nations, we aim to build a generation that fights with ideas and not weapons, respects one another, builds bridges instead of walls and shapes our shared future together.

Main Objective

To forge a dynamic frontline of young and articulate peacebuilders across Tanzania and Kenya by institutionalizing a culture of rigorous, respectful and solution-oriented dialogue, ensuring the nation's legacy of peace evolves and thrives in the 21st century.

Specific Objectives

Intellectual Empowerment

To train young minds in the arts of critical thinking, fact-checking and logical reasoning, protecting them against the rising tide of misinformation and digital radicalization.

Civic Empathy

To force participants to step into the shoes of their opponents, fostering deep-seated empathy, tolerance and an understanding of diverse Tanzanian and Kenyan cultures and socio-economic realities.

Policy Incubation

To provide a loud, clear microphone for the youth to propose actionable solutions to real-world challenges, such as climate-driven resource conflicts, youth unemployment, human trafficking and gender-based violence.

National Integration

To break down geographical and class barriers by bringing together students from rural and urban schools across the two countries for a shared intellectual journey.

Expected Outcomes

01

The TAKEPDC Alumni Network

The creation of a 2,000+ strong network of "Youth Peace Ambassadors" equipped to mediate local community disputes and lead civic initiatives.

02

The 2027 Youth Peace Charter

A compiled document of the most innovative policies and solutions debated during the championship, to be formally presented to the Ministry of Education and the Ministry of Youth.

03

Elevated Academic Standards

A noticeable spike in participating schools' performance in languages (English, French and Kiswahili), research methodologies, active communication and social sciences.

Our Contribution

01

National Security Framework

By equipping youth to resolve grievances through peace debate and respect for each other, the program acts as a grassroots deterrent against civil unrest and youth delinquency.

02

The Economy

Employers today seek soft skills such as communication, collaboration, creativity, critical thinking, leadership, flexibility, initiative, productivity, sociability, problem-solving and literacy skills. TAKEPDC 2027 accelerates the acquisition of these skills and makes Tanzanian and Kenyan youth more competitive in the East African and global job markets.

03

Global Leadership

The program directly operationalizes SDG 16 (Peace, Justice and Strong Institutions) and SDG 4 (Quality Education) and Aspirations four, five and seven of Agenda 2063: The Africa We Want. It positions Tanzania and Kenya as regional leaders in youth-driven peace initiatives.

Why Now?

In an era dominated by 15-second social media clips, the art of deep and nuanced conversation is fading. When young people lack the vocabulary or the platform to express their frustrations, those frustrations can manifest as apathy or conflict. As Tanzania and Kenya accelerate their economic and technological development, new societal frictions will inevitably arise.

TAKEPDC 2027 is a proactive and preventative investment. A peace debate championship requires participants to listen as fiercely as they speak. It teaches that one can attack an idea without attacking the person. There is no better time than now to inoculate our youth against intolerance and equip them with the diplomatic tools they need to lead tomorrow.

Dr. Martin Chamliho

The Living Smile International

The Living Smile International (LSI) is a non-governmental organization registered in Tanzania with registration number 00NGO/R/5698. Our vision is to be a center of excellence that conducts innovative research and training and builds capacity on peace, education, environment and human development for the better.

Since its inception, LSI has influenced thousands of students from across the country by providing basic school education equipment and engaging them in climate action. It has exposed more than 85 schools to the debate platform and successfully hosted the 2026 East Africa Peace Debate Championship (EAPDC 2026) at the Institute of Accountancy Arusha (IAA) in Arusha, Tanzania.
